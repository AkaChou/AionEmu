Thank you!<br> <br><br> <br><br><font color='CC0000'>Haramel<message></font> Guide<br> <br><br> Area 1: This is the instance entrance. Follow the passage to the end to pick up the Haramel quests. Continue onward to reach the first main area. There are two quests here: On your first run, collect Odium; On your second run, destroy the mine carts. There is a chest in the mine. Clear every enemy along the way. After defeating Drugelord Kakiti, follow the path to another quest NPC. <br><br> <br><br> Area 2—the lift room: Before using the lift, defeat the MuMu Mechanics and collect Lubricating Oil to open the chest upstairs. You also need the key carried by Keymaster MuMu Dang, who stands in front of the lift teleporter. The chest upstairs is required for one of the quests. <br><br> <br><br> Area 3: There are two quests here. On your second Haramel run, one requires collecting Dried Ginseng and the other requires burning Odella piles. You must also defeat Gray MuMu Ham. There is a chest in this room, and you need to collect Odella stems. Farther ahead, an NPC lets you turn in and accept quests. Another chest is located at the end of the room. Take the lift to the final area. <br><br> <br><br> Area 4: This is the final area of the instance. Many low-health enemies wait near the entrance; use area attacks to clear them quickly. Afterward, you will face Boss Nukiti. Defeat Nukiti to make Hamerun appear. During the Hamerun fight, he summons two enemies. Hamerun is invulnerable while they are alive, so defeat them first. When Hamerun dies, a cutscene plays and a treasure chest appears. Use the portal to leave the instance.<br><br> <br><br> ]]> Enjoy the game
